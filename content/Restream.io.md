Restream.io is a streaming platform we use for live streams.
The main benefit of using Restream.io vs. streaming directly to YouTube is it allows us to send our video signal to Restream and they can re-distribute it to many different destination such as YouTube, Facebook, Telegram, Instagram and many more.

It also makes it very simple to setup each live stream event by automating event creation.
