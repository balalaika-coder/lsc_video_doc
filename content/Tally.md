## Tally System Information
**Brand**: Hollyland
**Connectivity**: Wireless
**Lights**: Each light is wireless and has a battery or can be connected with a USB-C cable.

## Location
This system has a **main base station** which is connected in the sound booth for better signal.

## Power On
To power it on, HOLD power button for 3 seconds until you see the light glow green.
