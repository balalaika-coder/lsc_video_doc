- We have two of these Hollyland Wireless Receivers.
- One is used for rig and another one for the glide cam.
- They are either taped at the edge of stage or in case of glide cam, it is hiding inside the floor box. 
- When glide cam is in use, you can take it out of the floor box to improve signal strength.
